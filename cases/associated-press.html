Associated Press (1973)

Class-action lawsuit leading to five-year affirmative action plan

← Back to Cases

Case Overview

Filed: 1973 | Settlement: 1976-1977 | Lead Plaintiffs: Peggy Simpson, Virginia Sherlock, Shirley Christian, et al.

Women at the Associated Press filed a class-action suit challenging sex discrimination in hiring, promotion, and pay. The case resulted in a comprehensive five-year affirmative action plan and pay equity measures.

Key Plaintiffs

Peggy Simpson, Virginia Sherlock, and Shirley Christian played key roles in the AP suit and have contributed oral histories to various archives documenting their experiences.

Outcome and Settlement

The settlement included:

  • Five-year affirmative action plan
  • Pay equity measures
  • Hiring and promotion reforms

Like other organizations, the AP admitted no wrongdoing but agreed to substantial reforms.

Impact and Legacy

The AP case demonstrated that discrimination extended throughout the wire service industry and contributed to broader reforms in news gathering organizations nationwide.
