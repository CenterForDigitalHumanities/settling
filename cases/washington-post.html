Washington Post (1972)

Sex discrimination lawsuit leading to hiring and promotion reforms

← Back to Cases

Case Overview

Filed: 1972 | Settlement: 1975-1976 | Lead Plaintiffs: Claudia Levy, Karlyn Barker, et al.

Women at The Washington Post filed a class-action suit challenging patterns of sex discrimination in hiring, promotion, and pay. The case resulted in significant reforms to workplace practices.

Outcome and Settlement

The settlement included:

  • Hiring and promotion reforms
  • Commitments to address pay disparities
  • Monitoring mechanisms to track progress

Impact and Legacy

The Washington Post case contributed to industry-wide reforms and demonstrated that even news organizations with progressive reputations were not immune from civil rights enforcement.
