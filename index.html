Settling: Women Who Sued the News

An archive of stories from women who took legal action against news organizations

About This Archive

This archive documents the stories of women who have filed lawsuits against news organizations. These cases represent important moments in media accountability, women's rights, and the ongoing dialogue about responsible journalism.
