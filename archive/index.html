Document Archive

Court documents, photographs, press clippings, and personal papers

About the Archive

This digital archive supports both scholarly research and public education by providing access to primary source materials documenting the EEOC lawsuits against news organizations in the 1970s. Materials are organized using Dublin Core metadata standards and are available for research use.

Archive Collections

Court Documents

Complaints, depositions, settlement agreements, and consent decrees from federal and state courts.

  • Complaints and initial filings
  • Settlement agreements
  • Consent decrees and court orders

Photographs

Images documenting newsroom life, protests, and key figures from open-license collections.

  • Women in 1970s newsrooms
  • Women's rights protests and marches
  • Key figures in the movement

Press Clippings

News articles, editorials, and union publications documenting the lawsuits and their aftermath.

  • Contemporary news coverage
  • Editorial responses
  • Union newsletters and bulletins

Personal Papers

Memos, pay data, correspondence, and other materials shared by participants.

  • Internal memos and correspondence
  • Salary data and employment records
  • Personal reflections and notes

Oral History Materials

Transcripts, audio recordings, and annotated materials from oral history projects.

  • Interview transcripts
  • Audio recordings
  • Metadata and annotations

Teaching Materials

Lesson plans, discussion guides, and bibliographies for educators and researchers.

  • Lesson plans
  • Discussion guides
  • Annotated bibliographies

Metadata Standards

This archive adopts the Dublin Core Metadata Element Set for describing digital objects. Key metadata elements include:

  • Title
  • Creator
  • Subject
  • Description
  • Date (Original and Digital)
  • Format
  • Identifier (DOI, URL, or other persistent identifier)
  • Source
  • Rights
  • Contributing Institution

Image Sources and Rights

To ensure broad accessibility and compliance with copyright law, this archive uses images from open-license collections:

  • Library of Congress - Free to Use and Reuse Sets
  • Public Domain Image Archive - Aggregated public domain collections
  • Contributing Participants - Personal photographs and documents shared with permission
