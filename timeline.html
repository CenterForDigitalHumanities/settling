Timeline

Key events in the fight against workplace discrimination in journalism

Laws, once passed, do not magically change the workplace or society overnight. Instead, they are tested, adopted, and adapted rather slowly. The history of antidiscrimination law is not a linear progression toward its annihilation. Instead, it has ebbed and flowed and crashed.
