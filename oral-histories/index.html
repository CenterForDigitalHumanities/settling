Oral Histories

Documenting the voices behind the lawsuits

About the Oral History Project

This oral history project preserves the first-person accounts of the women who organized, litigated, and lived through the landmark sex discrimination lawsuits of the 1970s and 1980s. These interviews provide critical scholarly and historical context to the legal records, documenting the personal courage, coalition-building, and institutional resistance that defined this era.

The collection includes interviews with plaintiffs, co-counsel, and key figures from major cases at organizations such as Newsweek, The New York Times, The Washington Post, Reader's Digest, and the Associated Press.
