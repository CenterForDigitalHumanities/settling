How to read this

Every page on this site was originally generated by an AI assistant and is flagged with <meta name="generated" content="true">. When a page's content has been read and approved by a human, that flag is replaced with <meta name="reviewed" content="true">. This page lists the pages that are still flagged generated and therefore need review.

After reviewing a page, replace its generated meta tag with the reviewed tag and remove it from the table below.
